3. Our website

What we collect: nothing that identifies you unless you contact us (your email and message). Our hosting keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and safe.

Advertising and cookies: the website shows ads from Google AdSense and Monetag. These partners may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads, and may personalise them based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised Google ads at adssettings.google.com, and block cookies in your browser.

YouTube: parts of the site use YouTube API Services; videos are governed by the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

  • Supabase, Inc.: the news stories in Rajneeti; accounts and cloud save in GeoPolitics; website data (privacy policy)

5. How long we keep it

  • Rajneeti game data: on your device until you delete the game or its storage.
  • GeoPolitics accounts and progress: while your account exists; removed within 30 days of deletion.
  • Match records and player reports (GeoPolitics): 12 months.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and refund law requires.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to answer you.
